Each technique ends with a check (自检) and, where it matters, when it does not apply (不适用).

## Scene
- Name what is at stake — 信任、钱、秘密、安全、尊严 — and whether it is up or down at the start and at the end. Same at both ends: the scene only delivers information; cut it or move the information into a scene with conflict.
- A beat is an action and its reaction. A new beat starts when behaviour changes (试探 → 逼问 → 退让). The same tactic in new words is a repeated beat — the first sign of a weak scene.
- The turn is caused by an action or a revelation, comes late enough to be earned, and sends the story somewhere new.
- Enter late, leave early: start after the set-up, cut before the conflict is fully settled.
- Choose the place for pressure, not convenience. A confession whispered at a funeral reception costs more than one on a sofa. Before settling on 客厅、办公室、饭桌或车里, name three alternatives.
- 自检: 删掉这场，后面哪件事讲不通？都讲得通，就合并或删掉。
- 不适用: 有意的停顿、仪式或过渡场。它们管节奏，不管价值翻转——写清它的功能即可。

## Action lines
- Present tense, what is seen and heard, in the order the audience meets it. One visual idea per paragraph; a new paragraph often reads as a new shot.
- Specific nouns and verbs: not 很贵的衣服 but 熨得发亮的藏青西装; not 走过去 but 侧身挤过两张桌子.
- Inner states become behaviour or sound:
  - 弱：他很紧张。
  - 强：他第三次合上打火机盖，没点。
- Leave camera directions to text shots unless a framing carries meaning (特写：表盘停在 3:17).
- Small physical business carries a scene: 吃到一半的面、折了两次的车票、没拆的快递.
- 自检: 遮住对白只看动作，看得出这场谁占上风吗？

## Dialogue
- Plan before writing, for each speaker: what they say they want, what they actually want, why they cannot say it. When the two match, the exchange has no subtext — keep it only on purpose.
- Every turn does something: 试探、挡回、交换、佯退. Two people taking turns to hand the audience facts is an interrogation, not a scene.
- Answer the question that was not asked:
  - 弱：A：你昨晚去哪了？ B：去加班了，项目周五上线。
  - 强：A：你昨晚去哪了？ B：（把外卖袋放到桌上）趁热吃。
- Voices differ in sentence shape (短句或长句、反问或陈述), vocabulary, where each retreats under pressure, and what each refuses to discuss.
- Cut greetings, names in direct address, and lines confirming what was just shown. A silence or a gesture can finish a turn.
- A long speech needs a reason to be long (被逼到墙角、仪式、越编越大的谎) and a place where it breaks.
- 自检: 遮住署名，还分得清谁在说吗？分不清就改句式和回避方式，不要靠加口头禅。
- 不适用: 喜剧的重复梗、人物口癖、方言——重复是设计时，第二次要带来新的意思。

## Causality and escalation
- Join scenes with 因为……所以…… or 但是……, never 然后. Two adjacent scenes joined only by “and then” are missing a consequence.
- Escalate by changing what is at risk — power, information, a relationship, exposure, resources, time — not by repeating louder.
- Know what each turn is: a reveal changes what the audience understands; a reversal changes the direction of action; a payoff spends something set up earlier.
- A short film usually needs one central turn, not three acts. Episodes: see short-form.md.
- 自检: 用一句“因为…所以…”串起全部场次，串不上的那一环就是缺口。

## Props, plants and payoffs
- Plant what a payoff needs, visibly and early. Pay off every plant, or cut it.
- An object that carries meaning changes hands through someone's action, each time at a turn. In 《桃花扇》 the fan means something new at every step — a love token with a poem, a fan stained with blood, the stains painted into peach blossoms, a fan torn apart — and each change is someone's act.
- Track where things are and who holds them; a missing pickup or handover is a continuity error, not an interpretation.
- 自检: 这件道具哪一次露面删掉也不影响剧情？删掉那一次。

## Exploring options
When asked for directions or ideas, offer two or three that differ in mechanism — the protagonist's strategy, the source of opposition, the price — not in names, jobs or wording. For each, state what it promises the audience, what repeats, and what would end it. Write one version at a time once a direction is chosen. Options the person did not choose never become story facts.

## Rule strength
- Checkable (ids, references, tracked objects, quoted contradictions): fix or report; these block a handoff.
- Craft defaults (this file): follow unless the author's intent says otherwise, and say why when you depart.
- Taste (ending tone, hook type, point of view, style): the author decides; never block on it.
